# Company Leave Policy

This document details the leave entitlements, guidelines, and request procedures for all full-time and part-time employees.

## Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 25 paid vacation days per calendar year. Annual leave accrues monthly at a rate of 2.08 days per completed month of service. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement based on their scheduled weekly hours. Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be rolled over into the following calendar year, and rolled-over days must be used by March 31st or they will lapse. Employees must submit vacation requests at least 14 days in advance for leave spanning 5 consecutive business days or longer.

## Sick and Medical Leave
Employees receive 12 paid sick days per calendar year, available on January 1st of each year. Sick leave is intended for personal illness, medical appointments, or caring for an immediate family member who is ill. If an absence extends beyond 3 consecutive business days, the employee is required to provide a written medical certificate from a licensed physician upon returning to work. Unused sick leave does not roll over to subsequent years and is non-compensable upon separation of employment.

## Parental and Family Leave
We provide inclusive parental leave to support new parents welcoming a child through birth, adoption, or long-term foster placement. 
- Primary caregivers are entitled to 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave (100% of base salary).
- Secondary caregivers (non-primary / partner) are entitled to 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Employees become eligible for parental leave benefits after completing 6 months of continuous service prior to the expected date of birth or placement. Parental leave must be taken within the first 12 months following the child's birth or arrival. Employees may elect to take this leave continuously or split it into a maximum of 2 distinct blocks with prior managerial consent.

## Bereavement Leave
Employees are granted paid compassionate leave in the event of the loss of a family member:
- 5 consecutive paid business days for immediate family members (spouse, domestic partner, child, parent, or sibling).
- 3 consecutive paid business days for extended family members (grandparent, grandchild, aunt, uncle, niece, nephew, or parent-in-law).
An additional 2 unpaid days or 2 days deducted from annual leave may be approved if interstate or international travel is required to attend funeral services.

## Public Holidays and Floating Holidays
The company observes 11 designated official public holidays each year (including New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Day after Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day). In addition to statutory holidays, every employee receives 2 paid floating holidays per year on January 1st. Floating holidays can be utilized for cultural, religious, or personal observances and do not roll over across calendar years.

## Unpaid Leave and Sabbaticals
Employees with a minimum of 3 years of continuous tenure may apply for an unpaid sabbatical for personal development, research, or travel. Sabbaticals may range between 1 month and 6 months in duration. Sabbaticals require written approval from the department vice president and People Operations at least 60 days before the requested start date. Health insurance benefits remain subsidized during sabbaticals of up to 3 months.
