# Human Resources Procedures and Workplace Standards

This document establishes standard operating procedures for working arrangements, corporate expenses, performance evaluations, employee growth, and conflict resolution.

## Working Hours and Core Collaboration
Standard employment agreements define a baseline 40-hour work week, typically scheduled Monday through Friday.
- Core collaboration hours are set between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in the employee's designated primary time zone. All team members are expected to be reachable and available for synchronous meetings during this window.
- Outside core hours, employees may adopt flexible work hours between 7:00 AM and 7:00 PM with the concurrence of their department lead.
- Overtime for non-exempt hourly employees must receive pre-authorization from the direct supervisor and is paid at 1.5 times the standard hourly rate for hours in excess of 40 hours per week.

## Expense Reimbursement and Per Diems
Legitimate business expenses incurred while conducting company business are reimbursable through the automated finance portal:
- Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the transaction date. Claims submitted after 60 days require Chief Financial Officer sign-off.
- Itemized tax receipts are mandatory for all individual items exceeding $25.
- The standard daily meal per diem for domestic travel is capped at $75 per day ($20 breakfast, $25 lunch, $30 dinner). For designated high-cost metropolitan areas (San Francisco, New York City, London, Tokyo), the allowable meal per diem increases to $100 per day.
- Personal vehicle mileage for business travel is reimbursed at the federal statutory rate of $0.67 per mile.

## Performance Reviews and Promotion Cycles
Performance evaluations maintain transparent growth and fair compensation alignment:
- Formal performance appraisals take place semiannually in June (Mid-Year Review) and December (Annual Review).
- Employees and managers must complete written assessments within 10 business days following the cycle launch.
- Compensation adjustments and promotions resulting from annual reviews take effect on February 1st, while mid-year adjustments take effect on August 1st.
- An employee on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) is placed on a 60-day measurable benchmark cycle with bi-weekly progress checkpoints.

## Professional Development and Tuition Assistance
Continuous skill acquisition is supported through generous education budgets:
- All full-time employees who have completed at least 90 days of continuous employment are allocated an annual individual learning budget of $2,500. This budget can be used for textbooks, online bootcamps, technical certifications, and industry conferences.
- Degree-seeking tuition assistance of up to $5,000 per calendar year is available for accredited undergraduate or graduate coursework aligned with the employee's current or prospective role.
- Tuition assistance requires a grade of 'B' (3.0 GPA) or higher and carries a 12-month post-completion retention commitment.

## Grievance and Conflict Resolution
The company maintains an open-door environment and zero-tolerance policy for discrimination or harassment:
- Employees facing interpersonal friction or workplace grievances are encouraged to seek resolution with their immediate manager or People Partner within 14 calendar days of an incident.
- When formal grievances are submitted in writing, People Operations will assign an independent investigator within 5 business days and deliver a formal written finding within 20 business days.
- Confidential and anonymous reports regarding compliance, fraud, or unethical conduct may be submitted 24/7/365 via the EthicsPoint hotline (1-800-461-9330) or the secure ethics portal.
